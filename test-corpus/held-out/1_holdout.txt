Why the City Libary Should Stay Open on Sundays
Um, this essay explains why the city libary on Warren Street should unlock its doors on Sundays instead of sitting shut. Right now the buiding is dark all day and the parking lot holds nothing but a plow pile. Alot of families in my nieghborhood work Monday threw Friday, so the weekend is the only stretch of time they ever get. If the doors is locked on the one day they are free, than the libary is not really open to them.
My mom cleans offices six days a week and she can not drive me across town on a Tuesday afternoon. Alot of my friends parents is in the same spot, they drive a bus or stock shelfs or pull a double at the hospital. There cards are paid for out of there taxes, so it feels strange that the buiding only belongs to people with free weekdays. The librarian works the front desk it is packed at noon every Saturday becuase everybody is cramming a whole week into six hours.
Sunday is the day most kids actualy have time for a big project. A group essay or a science fair board takes more then one afternoon, and a cracked phone screen is not enough for real research. A kid can borrow calculators headphones novels and a hotspot for the week without paying a cent. The same kids who recieve free lunch are the ones who need the printer the most, closing on Sunday has a bad affect on every one of them.
Older people use the libary a diffrent way than teenagers do, you know, they come for the newspapers and for help with there phone. Mr. Alvarez down the hall is seventy eight and he does not own a computer, so every form he fills out he fills out at the reference desk. The volunteers is happy to sit with him and walk threw it line by line. Its not that seniors can not learn, its that nobody is there on a weekend to show them. Sunday hours would help teenagers grandparents nurses and cab drivers who never get a weekday off. It does not take much staff to keep that going, although the heating bill does climb a little.
