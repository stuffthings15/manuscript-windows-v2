A Review Of Marlow Street Park After The Renovation
This review is about the park on Marlow Street and whether the fourteen months of construction were worth it. My family has walked there since I was in second grade so I noticed every single change. The city spent alot of money and the new sign by the gate lists three seperate contractors. Um, I want to be fair and talk about what got better and also what got worse.
The entrence looks completely different now. The old gravel is gone and the front path is smooth it is wide enough for two strollers side by side. The benches near the gate got replaced to but there paint is already peeling in the corners. My mom said the old benches lasted twelve years so twelve months of peeling is not grate.
The playground is the part that changed the most. The slides was replaced with a taller set and the swings is hung higher off the ground. The new climbing wall is bigger then the old one and my little brother can not reach the second handhold yet. The playground equipment is new so nothing squeaks when you jump on it.
The bus is right at the corner now so kids from the north side can get there without a ride from a parent. The news about the reopening was on channel four for maybe ten seconds. Neither the map or the new sign is right about the closing hours, which matters if you are waiting for a ride in the dark. Parking got worse then it used to be because the lot only holds fourteen cars and the overflow spots became a garden bed.
You know the thing about the walking loop is that it goes around the pond twice which sounds long but it really is not and my dad times it every sunday morning he says it takes eleven minutes at a normal pace and the packed dirt they laid down means the stroller wheels do not sink anymore like they did in the spring. The upgrade gave families safety cleanliness supervision and a shaded place for grandparents to sit. Now there is a pavilion two grills a soccer field and a small dog run. One of the grills is broken already but the rest work fine. The picnic tables was bolted down this time so nobody drags them to the pond at night. The gate latch on the dog run is loose on purpose so it swings shut slowly, and there is a second gate behind it so nobody has to chase there dog out to the road. The pond is behind the fence on Halloway it freezes solid every winter.
The strip of grass along the north fence is only about ten feet wide but it still counts. The new path does not connect to the parking lot so people cut across the mud and now there is a brown line worn into the lawn. That is the one thing the designers clearly got wrong.
The lighting is the biggest improvement and the tall lamps had a real affect on how safe the loop feels after dark. The park is nicer now but it's fence along the creek is still bent in three places. Two of the screws on the swing set frame are lose and one bolt is missing entirely so I would not put a toddler on that end. Its going to need somebody checking the hardware every month or the city will loose the trust it just earned back. That sentence came out clumsy and I could not think of a better way to write it.
So overall I think the renovation was worth it, uh, mostly. The trees they planted are skinny now but in ten years the shade will be better then any canopy the city could buy.
