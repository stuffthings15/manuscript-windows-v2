Should Students Be Allowed To Use Phones In Class
This essay is about whether students should be allowed to keep there phones during class and what a fair rule would look like. Almost everybody in my grade owns one and they bring it to school anyway so the arguement is not really about the devices it is about what we do with them. Some teachers colect them in a basket by the door and other teachers do not care at all, which leaves every student guessing about what happens tomorrow.
The good side is easy to see. A phone gives students calculators dictionaries translators and reminders for the homework. Students is able to look up a hard word in seconds insted of waiting for the one class computer to finish loading. My cousin reads with an app on hers becuase the print in the textbook is to small for her, and it does not slow the class down at all.
The bad side is bigger then people want to admit. Phones is a distraction they buzz in the middle of a lesson and the whole row stops listening. One kid started a video with the sound all the way up during a quiz and the teacher lost ten minutes getting everybody back on task. Kids record each other in the hallway too and that is embarasing for the person being filmed and nobody ever asks them first.
Um so the next part is about the actual policy. Right now the rule is that a phone goes in the pocket chart at the door and you get it back at lunch, and the teacher grabs the you know basket on her way in. Honestly this paragraph is not saying much yet and I might move it later. Teachers was supposed to write a kid up for breaking the rule but most of them just gave a warning and moved on.
Phones also effect sleep and that shows up the next morning in first period. If somebody is up until two scrolling then they are useless by eight and no rule about class time is going to fix that. Some kids loose thier phone inside there own backpack and spend the first ten minutes of the period hunting for it. The affect on grades is small for a few students and huge for others so one rule for everybody is not really equal.
There is a fairness problem too. Not every family can buy a new phone and the kids with cracked old ones get laughed at, which is a whole seperate issue that I am only half explaining here. If a teacher catches you with it out for one second while your putting it away it still counts. That is harsh but at least it lands on everybody the same way.
A better plan is to teach kids when to use them. My science teacher lets us use the timer and take lab pictures and nobody messes around because she is definately walking the room the whole time. The instructions are short she says put them away and we put them away. Physics is hard enough without a video playing in the back row, and the survey our class did shows it is about the same at two other schools.
To be honest that last sentence sounded better in my head then it does written down. The point is that a total ban and a total free for all are both lazy answers to a real problem. A middle rule would be phones away during instruction and out for research time when the teacher says so. My homeroom tried it for a month and it worked ok untill a sub came in who did not know the system and everything fell apart again.
In the end students should be allowed to have phones in class but only when the teacher says its time. A school that bans them completly is just teaching kids to hide them better, and I guess that is a skill but not the one they came for. The policy needs to cover storage timing consequences and exceptions and every classroom needs the same version of it. Its not going to be perfect and some kids will still lose there focus, but a rule people actually follow beats a rule that nobody enforces.
